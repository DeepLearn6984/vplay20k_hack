\section*{VPlay20K -- W25Q32 flash map (4 MB)}

Main chip: VT369 (OneBus, NES 2.0 mapper 256, console type 0x0A).
Emulatable ROM: 16-byte NES 2.0 header + full dump (\texttt{vape\_full\_vt369.nes}).

\begin{bytefield}{24}
    % Empty (size = 0x040000)
    \memsection{3C0000}{3FFFFF}{3}{Empty (0xFF)} \\

    % Unknown data (size = 0x2D0000)
    \memsection{0F0000}{3BFFFF}{10}{\shortstack{Unknown data\\(no 6502 code; audio?)}} \\

    % Empty (size = 0x020000)
    \memsection{0D0000}{0EFFFF}{3}{Empty (0xFF)} \\

    % Harry Tetris PRG (size = 0x8000)
    \memsection{0C8000}{0CFFFF}{3}{Harry Tetris PRG (32 KB)} \\

    % Harry Tetris CHR (size = 0x2000)
    \memsection{0C6000}{0C7FFF}{3}{Harry Tetris CHR (8 KB)} \\

    % Pac-Man (size = 0x6000)
    \memsection{0C0000}{0C5FFF}{3}{Pac-Man (24 KB)} \\

    % F-22 (size = 0x40000)
    \memsection{080000}{0BFFFF}{5}{F-22 (256 KB, MMC3)} \\

    % Menu, fixed bank (size = 0x4000)
    \memsection{07C000}{07FFFF}{4}{\shortstack{Menu: fixed bank \$C000--\$FFFF\\vectors at 0x7FFFA}} \\

    % Menu, switchable banks (size = 0x16000)
    \memsection{066000}{07BFFF}{4}{Menu: code banks} \\

    % Test program (size = 0x6000)
    \memsection{060000}{065FFF}{3}{Test program (24 KB)} \\

    % Menu graphics (size = 0x60000)
    \memsection{000000}{05FFFF}{6}{\shortstack{Menu graphics\\8bpp tiles (256 colours)}} \\
\end{bytefield}

\subsection*{Menu code (fixed bank, CPU addresses)}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Address & Role \\
\midrule
\texttt{\$EEFB} / \texttt{\$D520} / \texttt{\$D6AA} & RESET / NMI / IRQ \\
\texttt{\$F113} & Game launcher (banks \$4107--\$410A, jump to RAM) \\
\texttt{\$DD07} + table \texttt{\$DB08} & LCD init, 160$\times$128, 16-bit colour \\
\texttt{\$E189}--\texttt{\$E35A} & Bit-banged I2C (\$4150/\$4152, read \$4153), device 0x40 \\
\texttt{\$CC11} + table \texttt{\$E372} & I2C challenge-response (anti-clone?), ``Wellminds Technology'' \\
\texttt{\$D3A9} / \texttt{\$D44C} & BEAST / REGULAR confirmation \\
\texttt{\$EF74} & Reads MCU status at boot \\
\texttt{\$D0CC} & Power-down (\$4175 = \$19) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection*{Link to the vape microcontroller (port \texttt{\$41EA}/\texttt{\$41EB})}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Direction & Bits & Meaning \\
\midrule
VT369 $\rightarrow$ MCU & bit 6 & Mode: 0 = BEAST (25 W), 1 = REGULAR \\
VT369 $\rightarrow$ MCU & bit 7 & Unknown (\$0D27) \\
MCU $\rightarrow$ VT369 & bits 5--4 = \texttt{01} & Battery, 0--5 (\$0D30) \\
MCU $\rightarrow$ VT369 & bits 5--4 = \texttt{11} & E-liquid, 0--10 (\$0D2F) \\
MCU $\rightarrow$ VT369 & bit 5 at boot & Device status; bit 3 = saved mode \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The 25 W, 3.5 V and 0.5 $\Omega$ values on the BEAST screen are fixed images:
the actual heating power is set by the microcontroller, not by the VT369.
